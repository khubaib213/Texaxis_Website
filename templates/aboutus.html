Our Story

TEXAXIS supplies textile processing chemicals and enzymes to manufacturers across Pakistan. We focus on reliable product quality, practical technical support, and consistent supply from our Lahore head office.

Mission

Supply high-quality textile processing chemicals and enzymes that improve efficiency, support responsible processing, and deliver clear value to mills.

Vision

Be Pakistan's most trusted partner for textile processing solutions — known for product quality, technical support, and reliable supply.

Values

Integrity, practical innovation, customer focus, and environmental responsibility guide how we work with mills and suppliers.

Our Expertise

Our key founders are highly experienced technical professionals with over 18 years of experience in textile processing and sales of textile enzymes and auxiliaries throughout Pakistan.

We specialize in supplying premium-quality textile processing chemicals to manufacturers across all textile sectors, ensuring optimal results and operational excellence.

Our Journey

From founding in Lahore to coverage across Pakistan’s textile hubs — a clear path of steady growth.

2017

Foundation & Vision

TEXAXIS (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED was established with a vision to revolutionize the textile chemical supply industry in Pakistan. Started operations in Lahore with a dedicated team of experienced professionals.

2018

Market Expansion

Expanded our product portfolio to include industrial enzymes and specialized auxiliaries. Established strong partnerships with leading international suppliers and grew our customer base across Punjab region.

2019

Faisalabad Coverage

Extended dedicated coverage into Faisalabad — Pakistan’s textile manufacturing hub — with technical support and supply coordinated from our Lahore head office.

2020

Innovation & Quality Standards

Despite global challenges, strengthened our commitment to quality. Implemented advanced inventory management systems and achieved significant milestones in customer satisfaction and product reliability.

2021

Karachi Coverage

Expanded coverage into Karachi for Sindh mills and port-linked logistics, with delivery and customer service coordinated through our Lahore head office.

2022

Digital Operations

Invested in digital infrastructure to streamline order handling, inventory visibility, and faster customer response across our supply network.

2023

Sustainable Solutions

Focused on eco-friendly and sustainable textile chemical solutions. Partnered with environmentally conscious suppliers and introduced greener alternatives for textile processing.

2024

Industry Leadership

Strengthened our position as a trusted supplier in Pakistan's textile chemical industry with deeper technical support and a customer-first approach.

2026 & Beyond

Continued Growth

Expanding product coverage and technical capability while remaining Pakistan's reliable partner for textile processing chemicals and enzymes.

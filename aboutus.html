About TEXAXIS

Building Excellence in Textile Chemicals Since 2017

TEXAXIS (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED

Our Story

TEXAXIS is a forward-thinking trading company specializing in textile processing chemicals and enzymes across all textile sectors in Pakistan. Founded with a vision to revolutionize the textile processing industry, we have established ourselves as a trusted partner for textile manufacturers throughout the country.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality textile processing chemicals and enzymes that enhance production efficiency, reduce environmental impact, and deliver exceptional value to our clients.

Our Vision

To become Pakistan's leading supplier of innovative textile processing solutions, setting industry standards for quality, service, and sustainability.

Our Values

Integrity, innovation, customer focus, and environmental responsibility guide every decision we make and every relationship we build.

Our Expertise

Our key founders are highly experienced technical professionals with over 18 years of experience in textile processing and sales of textile enzymes and auxiliaries throughout Pakistan.

We specialize in supplying premium-quality textile processing chemicals to manufacturers across all textile sectors, ensuring optimal results and operational excellence.

Our Journey

Building excellence in textile chemicals since 2017

2017

Foundation & Vision

TEXAXIS (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED was established with a vision to revolutionize the textile chemical supply industry in Pakistan. Started operations in Lahore with a dedicated team of experienced professionals.

2018

Market Expansion

Expanded our product portfolio to include industrial enzymes and specialized auxiliaries. Established strong partnerships with leading international suppliers and grew our customer base across Punjab region.

2019

Faisalabad Office Launch

Opened our Faisalabad office to better serve the heart of Pakistan's textile manufacturing industry. Introduced technical support services and on-site consultation for our valued clients.

2020

Innovation & Quality Standards

Despite global challenges, strengthened our commitment to quality. Implemented advanced inventory management systems and achieved significant milestones in customer satisfaction and product reliability.

2021

Karachi Operations

Established strategic presence in Karachi, Pakistan's largest port city. Enhanced our supply chain efficiency ensuring faster delivery times and expanded our reach to Sindh's textile manufacturers.

2022

Digital Transformation

Invested in digital infrastructure to streamline operations. Launched customer portal for order tracking and introduced data-driven solutions for inventory management and customer service excellence.

2023

Sustainable Solutions

Focused on eco-friendly and sustainable textile chemical solutions. Partnered with environmentally conscious suppliers and introduced green chemistry alternatives for textile processing.

2024

Industry Leadership

Recognized as a leading supplier in Pakistan's textile chemical industry. Expanded team capabilities with technical experts and strengthened our position through innovation and customer-first approach.

2025 & Beyond

Future Growth

Continuing our journey of excellence with plans for further expansion, technological advancement, and maintaining our commitment to being Pakistan's most trusted partner in textile processing solutions.

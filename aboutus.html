Our Story

TEXAXIS is a forward-thinking trading company specializing in textile processing chemicals and enzymes across all textile sectors in Pakistan. Founded with a vision to revolutionize the textile processing industry, we have established ourselves as a trusted partner for textile manufacturers throughout the country.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality textile processing chemicals and enzymes that enhance production efficiency, reduce environmental impact, and deliver exceptional value to our clients.

Our Vision

To become Pakistan's leading supplier of innovative textile processing solutions, setting industry standards for quality, service, and sustainability.

Our Values

Integrity, innovation, customer focus, and environmental responsibility guide every decision we make and every relationship we build.

Our Expertise

Our key founders are highly experienced technical professionals with over 18 years of experience in textile processing and sales of textile enzymes and auxiliaries throughout Pakistan.

We specialize in supplying premium-quality textile processing chemicals to manufacturers across all textile sectors, ensuring optimal results and operational excellence.
